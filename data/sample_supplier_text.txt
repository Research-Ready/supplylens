SUPPLIER PROFILE: ZF Friedrichshafen AG
Country: Germany
Sector: Automotive Components
Tier: 1 (direct supplier to OEMs)
Annual Revenue: ~43 billion EUR
Employees: ~165,000 worldwide

PRODUCTS SUPPLIED TO SCANIA:
- 12-speed TraXon automatic gearboxes for heavy trucks
- ZF EcoMat automatic transmissions for buses
- Driveline components and axle systems

SUPPLY CHAIN (Tier 2):
ZF sources the following critical materials and components:
1. Steel billets and forgings: Saarstahl AG (Saarbrücken, Germany)
   - High-strength alloy steel for gear shafts
   - Volume: ~180,000 tons/year
2. Aluminium housings: Martinrea Honsel (Meschede, Germany)
   - Die-cast aluminium transmission housings
3. Electronic control units: Continental AG (Hannover, Germany)
   - Transmission control modules (TCM)
   - Sensors and actuators
4. Bearings: Schaeffler AG (Herzogenaurach, Germany)
   - INA brand needle bearings for planetary gear sets
5. Seals and gaskets: Freudenberg (Weinheim, Germany)
   - Elastomer seals for gearbox assemblies

CERTIFICATIONS:
- ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management)
- IATF 16949:2016 (Automotive Quality)
- ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental)
- REACH compliant

RISK FLAGS:
- SINGLE_SOURCE: Steel supply concentrated at Saarstahl (backup: ArcelorMittal)
- GEOPOLITICAL_RISK: Some rare earth elements in electronics from CN

SUPPLIER PROFILE: SSAB AB
Country: Sweden
Sector: Steel Manufacturing
Tier: 1 (direct supplier to Scania)
Annual Revenue: ~8 billion EUR

PRODUCTS SUPPLIED TO SCANIA:
- Hardox wear plate for tipper bodies
- Strenx high-strength structural steel for chassis rails
- Docol advanced high-strength steel for cab components

SUPPLY CHAIN (Tier 2):
1. Iron ore pellets: LKAB (Kiruna, Sweden)
   - LKAB iron ore pellets (DR-grade for electric arc furnace)
   - Annual volume: ~5 million tons
2. Coking coal: Carrington Coal (Australia) + Tata Steel (NL)
   - For blast furnace operations (SSAB transitioning to hydrogen DRI)
3. Limestone: Nordkalk (Pargas, Finland)
   - Slag-forming agent in steelmaking
4. Scrap steel: Various European scrap collectors
   - 25-30% recycled content in EAF production
5. Hydrogen (future): Vattenfall/H2GS joint venture
   - Green hydrogen for HYBRIT fossil-free steel (pilot phase)
6. Oxygen: Air Liquide (France)
   - Industrial oxygen for steelmaking

SUSTAINABILITY NOTE:
SSAB HYBRIT pilot plant in Luleå producing fossil-free steel via direct reduced iron (DRI)
using green hydrogen. Scania committed to fossil-free steel in chassis from 2030.
